ABOUT ME
I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the McWilliams School of Biomedical Informatics, UTHealth Houston. My research focuses on biomedical artificial intelligence, computational biology, and clinical informatics, with particular interests in large language models (LLMs), AI agents, prompt optimization, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and multimodal AI. I also work with single-cell RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, and multi-omics data to investigate disease-associated cellular and regulatory mechanisms and develop AI-driven solutions for biomedical discovery and clinical applications.
